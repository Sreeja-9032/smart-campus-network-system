📖 Laboratory Guide: Capturing Campus Traffic with Wireshark

  1. Launch Wireshark on your workstation and select your active network adapter (e.g., Ethernet or Wi-Fi).
  2. Enter a display filter in the filter bar, e.g.: ip.addr == 192.168.1.10 || icmp || tcp.port == 8080.
  3. Generate traffic by pinging a campus department server or accessing a local web portal.
  4. Click the red square button to Stop Packet Capture.
  5. In Wireshark, navigate to File ➜ Export Packet Dissections ➜ As CSV... and save the file.
  6. Click Import Wireshark CSV above and paste the contents to visualize and analyze your live capture!

Mathematical Formulas & Derivations

                

                    

                        
Packet Loss

                        
Packet Loss = Sent Packets - Received Packets

                        
Calculated from trace event markers ('+' for enqueue, 'r' for receive, 'd' for drop).

                    

                    

                        
Packet Delivery Ratio (PDR)

                        
PDR (%) = (Received Packets / Sent Packets) × 100

                        
Proportion of packets successfully delivered to destination node.

                    

                    

                        
Throughput

                        
Throughput = Total Received Bits / Simulation Time

                        
Rate of successful message delivery over the campus channel (Mbps).

                    

                    

                        
Average End-to-End Delay

                        
Avg Delay = Σ(T_recv - T_send) / Total Received Packets

                        
Average time taken for a packet to traverse all hops from sender to receiver.
